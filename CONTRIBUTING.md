# CONTRIBUTING
> "I" refers to [DocilElm](https://github.com/DocilElm/) the owner of [Devonian](https://github.com/Synnerz/devonian) and [Synnerz](https://github.com/Synnerz) organization

We appreciate you wanting to contribute to [Devonian](https://github.com/Synnerz/devonian).

## Do We Accept Pull Requests?
This project is part of my hobby and thus i try to take care as much as possible of its image (maybe a little too much).

sadly for outside contributors, i do not like people with weird background or those who i don't know.

I also like features and code in general to be done a very specific way and i don't feel like wasting the time of others to teach them the way that they should code.

## What About BugFixes?
My thought behind bugfixes (mostly related to those which can be fixed in <5 lines)
has always been that of
> i'm the developer why would i waste the time of someone else to fix a simple and/or easy bug

So if you have a bug and are thinking to fix it, please refrain from doing so and open a bug report in either our [Discord Server](https://discord.gg/DgRsG2PZS9) or in the [Issues](https://github.com/Synnerz/devonian/issues) section

## End
As of 6/1/2026 i have decided to make Pull Requests contributors-only, essentially closing off pull requests to the public.

Once again, we really appreciate your want to contribute to our project.
